This template configured the following pins and peripherals:


RB10: GPIO output, for LED, active low
RB11: GPIO output, for LED, active low
RB12: GPIO output, for LED, active low

RB5: GPIO input, for button, active low

RB2: UART peripheral, UART_RX (receive)
RB3: UART peripheral, UART_TX (transmit)
UART baudrate: 19200

Program behavior:
1. Transmit "UART demo" via UART TX (RB3)
2. Echo ASCII char received from UART RX (RB2)
3. Toggle GPIO RB12 (for LED) at 1 Hz
