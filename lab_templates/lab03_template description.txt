This template configured the following pins and peripherals:

RB10: OC1 output, for LED
RB11: OC2 output, for LED
RB12: OC3 output, for LED

Program behavior:
1. LED at full brightness upon MCU reset
2. LED intensity dims linearly
3. LED reaches minimum brightness
4. repeat step 1.
